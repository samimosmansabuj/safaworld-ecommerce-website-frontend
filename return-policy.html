Full Return & Refund Policy
🗓️ Last updated: January 2025

1. Overview

At Safa World, we take great pride in the quality of our products. We want every customer to be fully satisfied with their purchase. If for any reason you are not satisfied, this policy outlines the process and conditions for returns, exchanges, and refunds.

Please read this policy carefully before placing an order. By making a purchase on our website, you agree to the terms set out below.

2. Eligibility for Returns

A return may be eligible under the following circumstances:

  • The item was received in a damaged or defective condition
  • The wrong item was delivered (different from what was ordered)
  • The item is significantly different from its description on the website

Return requests must be initiated within [X] days of receiving your order. Requests made after this period may not be accepted.

3. Return Conditions

To be eligible for a return, the item must meet all of the following conditions:

  • The product must be unused and in the same condition in which it was received
  • The product must be in its original packaging
  • All accessories, tags, and documentation included with the product must be returned
  • A receipt or proof of purchase (order number or order confirmation) must be provided

4. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Items that have been used, assembled, or installed
  • Items that have been damaged by the customer after delivery
  • Items returned without original packaging
  • Items purchased during special final-sale promotions (if applicable and clearly stated at time of purchase)
  • Customized or personalized products (if applicable)

5. How to Request a Return

To initiate a return, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact us via our Contact Us page, WhatsApp, or phone.
  2. Provide your order number, the item(s) you wish to return, and the reason for the return.
  3. Attach clear photographs of the item showing the issue (for damaged/defective/wrong item returns).
  4. Our team will review your request and respond within [X] business days.
  5. If approved, we will guide you through the return shipment process.

6. Exchange Policy

We support exchanges for products that were received damaged, defective, or in the wrong variant/model. Exchanges are subject to product availability. If the requested replacement is not available, we will offer a full refund instead.

To request an exchange, please follow the same process as a return request and specify that you would prefer an exchange over a refund.

7. Refund Process

Once your return is received and inspected by our team, we will notify you of the status of your refund.

  • If approved, your refund will be processed and credited back to your original payment method or delivered as a cash refund, depending on the payment method used.
  • For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds may be issued via mobile banking (bKash/Nagad) or bank transfer.

8. Refund Timeline

After your return has been approved and the item received, please allow [X]–[X] business days for the refund to be processed. Additional time may be required depending on your bank or mobile banking provider.

9. Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive a product that is damaged or defective, please contact us immediately (within 24–48 hours of receiving the item) and provide:

  • Your order number
  • A clear description of the damage or defect
  • Photographs showing the damage or defect
  • Photograph of the original packaging (if applicable)

We will prioritize your case and work to resolve it as quickly as possible — either with a replacement or a full refund.

10. Wrong Product Received

If you receive a product that is different from what you ordered, please contact us within [X] days of delivery. We will arrange for the correct item to be sent or issue a full refund, including any applicable delivery charges.

11. Shipping & Return Charges

In cases of damaged, defective, or wrong items, Safa World will cover the return shipping charges.

For returns due to other reasons (e.g., change of mind, if applicable under our policy), the customer may be responsible for return shipping costs. Please contact us to confirm before sending any items back.

12. Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled before they are dispatched. Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled — you will need to follow the return process instead.

To cancel an order, please contact us as soon as possible via Contact Us, WhatsApp, or phone with your order number.
